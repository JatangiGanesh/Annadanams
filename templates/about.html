About Vinayaka Chavithi Annadanam Finder

Promoting social service, encouraging food donation, and eliminating food wastage
Divine Mission & Social Service

"సర్వదానాలలో అన్నదానం మిన్న - అన్నదాత సుఖీభవ"

Vinayaka Chavithi (Ganesh Chaturthi) is celebrated with immense devotion and enthusiasm across millions of mandapams, temples, and communities for 9 to 11 days. During this divine period, thousands of generous donors, youth associations, and spiritual trusts organize Annadanam (free food distribution).

However, many devotees, travelers, daily wage workers, students, and families are unaware of where and when Annadanam is being served in their nearby area. At the same time, organizers often struggle with estimating visitor count, leading either to shortage or heartbreaking food wastage.

Our Core Objective: To provide a seamless, free, community-powered digital platform connecting food donors with devotees and people in need, equipped with exact location tracking and 1-click Google Maps navigation.

9 Days Navaratri Significance

Traditional sacred offerings during Vinayaka Chavithi

Day 1 (Chavithi): Ganesha Prathishta & Modaka Prasadam
Day 2-4: Undrallu, Kudumulu & Chintapandu Pulihara
Day 5-7: Bellam Pongali, Sundal, Daddojanam & Fruits
Day 8 (Ashtami): Maha Naivedyam & Laddu Prasadam
Day 9 (Navami): Maha Annadanam Meals & Visarjan Seva
Easy Location Access

Visitors easily discover nearest Annadanam venues by district, area, and day with direct Google Maps directions.

Zero Food Wastage

Real-time status updates allow donors to broadcast when food is serving or exhausted, optimizing food distribution.

Community Empowerment

Encourages youth clubs, temples, and families to register their seva and serve nutritious food to maximum devotees.

Guidelines for Annadanam Donors & Volunteers

Maintain Cleanliness & Hygiene: Wear caps/aprons, ensure clean drinking water stations, and cover food items.
Use Eco-Friendly Dining Plates: Prefer traditional banana plantain leaves or areca nut leaf plates rather than single-use plastics.
Separate Lines for Elderly & Children: Provide priority seating and assistance for senior citizens and differently-abled devotees.
Keep Status Updated: Update your event status to "Serving Now" when serving begins and "Exhausted" once distribution concludes.
